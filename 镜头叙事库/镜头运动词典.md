# 镜头运动词典

> 镜头运动类型 + 自然语序模板。中英文版本同源，便于跨模式（Seedance 中文 / h3 英文）复用。

## 11 种运动类型

| 中文 | 英文（自然语序） | 含义 |
|---|---|---|
| 推近 | The camera pushes in ... | 焦距变化，相机本身不动 |
| 拉远 | The camera pulls out ... | 同上，反向 |
| 推入 | The camera pushes in ... | 相机向前移动 |
| 拉出 | The camera pulls out ... | 相机向后移动 |
| 左摇 | The camera pans left ... | 相机位置不动，镜头水平左转 |
| 右摇 | The camera pans right ... | 同上，右转 |
| 横移左 | The camera trucks left ... | 相机水平左移 |
| 横移右 | The camera trucks right ... | 相机水平右移 |
| 上仰 | The camera tilts up ... | 镜头垂直上转 |
| 下俯 | The camera tilts down ... | 镜头垂直下转 |
| 上升 | The camera pedestals up ... | 整机向上 |
| 下降 | The camera pedestals down ... | 整机向下 |
| 弧线 | The camera arcs ... | 绕主体运动 |
| 跟踪 | The camera tracks ... | 跟随运动主体 |
| 静止 | The camera holds a static shot ... | 不动 |
| 轻晃 | The camera shakes slightly ... | 轻微抖动 |
| 强晃 | The camera shakes strongly ... | 大幅抖动 |
| POV | POV shot | 主体视角 |
| 顺时针旋转 | The camera rolls clockwise ... | 绕光轴顺时针 |
| 逆时针旋转 | The camera rolls counterclockwise ... | 绕光轴逆时针 |

## 幅度修饰

- `with small amplitude` — 小幅
- `with large amplitude` — 大幅
- 默认中等幅度（可省略）

## 速度修饰

- `at slow speed` — 慢
- `at fast speed` — 快
- 默认中等速度（可省略）

## 写作模板（推荐直接复用）

```
The camera pushes in with small amplitude at slow speed toward [目标].
The camera pans right with small amplitude at fast speed, revealing [新元素].
The camera holds a static shot as [主体动作].
The camera arcs slightly to the right around <Subject 1>.
The camera tilts up with small amplitude at slow speed, framing her face.
The camera shakes slightly with handheld breathing rhythm throughout.
```

## 不使用

- ❌ `Zoom In` / `Zoom Out`（违反 1× 主摄原则）
- ❌ 组合多种运动类型在同一秒内
- ❌ `with large amplitude` 在 10 秒内（除非明确需要）

## 与本工程约束的对应

- 1× 主摄 → 不使用 Zoom
- 真实手机景深 → 偶尔用 `with small amplitude` 暗示对焦迟疑
- 手持微晃 → 用 `The camera holds a static shot with handheld micro-shake and breathing rhythm`
